Robot Manufactering

The engineering process is long and we had to completely rebuild the robot from the ground up. So my first job as a Frame Designer was to start designing a new way to hold the electronics of the robot. Additionally, I also had to import and design the compnents we were going to buy to have an accurate model to work from. Next was do a design review to make sure we are meeting the competition standards and our design is good. After that one more round of 3d modeling and we start buying materials. Specifically, HPV to water jet the frame out off. Then we assembled the design following the 3d model and after some sanding to fix tolerences, we had a fully functional frame we now are testing in the pool to make sure it will work.

Learning

I learned quite a lot from being a part of robotics at Northwestern, the biggest lessons for me though was how to work in a team, and the significance of deadlines. The former speaks for itself but the latter I learned because when designing, I couldn't continue unless someone had finished their part and vice versa, so we had to meet deadlines to ensure we could finish the robot on time.
